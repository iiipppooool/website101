Privacy notice

Version 9.2  ·  Effective 1 September 2026

This website

Forms on this website store what you submit, the time, and your country, so that the site's creator can count sign-ups. IP addresses are not stored and nothing is shared or sold. To have an entry removed, email privacy@engramyx.org. The sections below describe the Lattice service.

1. Who we are

Engramyx Neurotechnologies AG ("Engramyx", "we") is the controller of personal data processed through the Lattice implant, the Engramyx app and this website. You can contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@engramyx.org.

2. What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you register, such as your name, contact details and medical history, and information generated by your Lattice.

  • Captured memories, including sensory content, emotional scores and inner speech
  • Device telemetry, location and sleep data
  • Motor cortex activity and, where Motor Link is enabled, motor instructions sent to you
  • Information about you contained in other users' memories

3. How we use it

We use your data to provide the service, to keep the Lattice safe and working, to meet our legal obligations, and to improve our products. Improvement includes training machine-learning models on stored memories.

Where we rely on consent, that consent was given at implantation and covers the purposes listed in this notice as amended from time to time.

4. Who we share it with

We share data with your Guardian, with clinical partners, with service providers acting on our instructions, and with authorities where the law requires it. Where your Lattice was provided by an employer, school or insurer, that organisation is your Guardian and receives the same access.

We do not sell personal data. We may license aggregated or individual memories to partners under agreements that prohibit resale.

5. How long we keep it

Memories are kept for the lifetime of your account and for a reasonable period afterwards. Deleting a memory removes it from your access. Archival copies are retained for safety, research and legal purposes.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to object to certain uses. Requests are made through the app or in writing.

Where an account has a Guardian, requests must be approved by the Guardian. We may decline a request where fulfilling it would cause distress, including distress caused by learning what the data contains.

7. International transfers

Data is stored in Iceland, Switzerland and Singapore and may be processed elsewhere under appropriate safeguards.

8. Changes to this notice

We update this notice from time to time. The current version always applies. Where a change is significant we will make sure you are comfortable with it.

Terms of service

Version 9.2  ·  Effective 1 September 2026

1. The service

These terms govern your use of the Lattice implant, the Engramyx app and related services. By registering, attending a screening or undergoing implantation you agree to them.

2. Eligibility

You must be 18 or over to register for yourself. People under 18, and adults who lack capacity, may be registered by a parent, guardian or other authorised person, who becomes their Guardian.

3. The device

The Lattice is a permanent implant. The ceramic shell may be removed by an Engramyx clinician. The thread mesh integrates with surrounding tissue and is not designed to be removed. The Lattice has no off state.

4. Guardians

A Guardian may view, strengthen, edit and delete memories on an account, and may start Motor Link sessions, within the permissions set for that account. Changes to a Guardian's permissions, including removal of the Guardian, take effect when the Guardian approves them.

5. Motor Link

Motor Link is enabled by default on Generation 3 devices. By accepting these terms you consent to motor instructions issued by you, by your Guardian and by Engramyx for maintenance and safety purposes. Sessions end when the person who started them ends them.

6. Fees

Fees are set out on our pricing page. If a payment is missed, capture continues and replay is suspended until the balance is cleared.

7. Changes

We may change these terms and the features of the service. Consent given at implantation extends to future features. Continued use of the Lattice constitutes acceptance, and use of the Lattice is continuous.

8. Ending the agreement

You may ask to close your account at any time. Closure requires an exit interview at an Engramyx clinic. After closure your stored memories remain with Engramyx and your access to them ends.

9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, Engramyx is not liable for actions taken by a Guardian, for the content of any memory, or for the location or condition of a user following a Motor Link session.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Switzerland. Disputes are resolved by confidential arbitration in Zug. Participants agree that their memory of the proceedings may be adjusted to protect confidentiality.
