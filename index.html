Capabilities01 / 06

One implant.
Six things your mind could never do.

Each one is optional. Most people switch them all on within a month.

01 — Capture

Every moment, as it forms.

The Lattice reads the hippocampus directly and writes each memory to your private cloud in real time. Sight, sound, touch, taste, and what you were thinking.

02 — Strengthen

Choose what stays vivid.

Flag a memory before bed. While you sleep, the Lattice replays it through slow-wave cycles until it is as sharp as the day it happened.

03 — Recall

Go back. Properly.

Replay any stored memory in full sensory detail. Not a video of it. The memory itself, running again on the hardware it was recorded from.

  • 14 Mar   Dinner at Aldo's
  • 02 Jun   The argument
  • 19 Aug   First day, new office
  • 03 Nov   The phone call
  • 24 Dec   Snow on the M40
04 — Edit

Soften. Fade. Delete.

Some memories don't earn their place. Turn down the ones that hurt, or remove them completely. You won't notice the gap. That's the point.

05 — Remote

Someone to look after it for you.

Appoint an authorised Guardian: a parent, a partner, an employer. They manage your memories from their own phone, so you don't have to think about it.
