How it works

The journey of one memory.

It is 07:42. Someone you love laughs at something you said. Here is what happens next, in the 1.2 seconds before you have even finished smiling.

Memory #48,201,337 T + 0.000 s
01 — Formation

A neuron fires.

In the hippocampus, a few thousand cells fire together in a pattern that has never occurred before. That pattern is the memory. Left alone, most of it would be gone by Thursday.

  • Region CA1, left hippocampus
  • Signal −70 mV → +40 mV
02 — Capture

The Lattice is already listening.

1,024 threads, each a tenth the width of a hair, pick up the pattern as it happens. The EX-3 processor encodes it before the signal has reached the end of the axon.

  • Latency 0.011 s
  • Listening 24 hours a day
03 — Storage

It leaves your head.

The encoded memory is sent to the Engramyx cloud and written to three regions at once. It is encrypted so that only you, your Guardian, and Engramyx can open it.

  • Regions Reykjavík · Zug · Singapore
  • Retention Indefinite
04 — Access

It appears in the app.

Titled, tagged and scored for emotional weight. By the time you notice you are happy, the moment is already filed, searchable, and ready to replay.

  • Total time 1.204 s
  • Visible to You + 1 Guardian
05 — Return path

And the line runs both ways.

Anything that travels up can travel down. Edits, reinforcements and instructions reach the Lattice by the same route, and the Lattice writes them back into the tissue. It is considerably faster in this direction.

  • Return latency 0.004 s
  • Confirmation Not required

Questions

No. Implantation takes around 40 minutes under local anaesthetic and you go home the same day. Most people say they don't remember it at all.

The Lattice has no off state, because a gap in capture is a gap in you. You can pause playback at any time from the app.

You, and anyone you have appointed as a Guardian. Engramyx staff may review memories to maintain service quality, to comply with lawful requests, and to train our models.

If your Lattice was provided by an employer, school or insurer, they are your Guardian by default.

A well-made edit is one you cannot detect. Every change is recorded in your consent ledger, which you can request in writing. Requests are themselves memories and may be edited.

The shell can be removed in a short procedure. The thread mesh integrates with surrounding tissue within 90 days and is designed to remain in place. After removal the mesh stays active in a low-power state.

Your stored memories remain safely in the Engramyx cloud. Your access to them ends at the close of your billing period.
