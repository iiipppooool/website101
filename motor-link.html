      Safeguards

      Designed so that nothing can go wrong for the operator.

      1. Consent is collected onceAt implantation, as part of the standard terms. It does not need to be asked for again.
      2. Sessions end when the operator ends themA session cannot be interrupted by the subject, by pain, or by loss of connection. On disconnection the last instruction repeats.2
      3. No memory of the session is requiredBy default, sessions are excluded from capture. Soreness, injuries and changes of location are explained by a short covering memory.
      4. Force limitsMotor Link will not exceed the structural tolerance of the subject's joints unless the operator selects Priority mode.
      5. Subject overrideA way for the subject to stop a session is on our roadmap. We have no date for it.

      1 Restoration may be delayed where a session is paused rather than ended. Paused sessions have no time limit.   2 Walking programmes continue in the last heading until signal is restored. Engramyx is not liable for where the subject is found.
