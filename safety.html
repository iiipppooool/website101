Your mind is safe with us.

Nothing matters more to us than your trust. That is why every promise on this page is reviewed by our legal team and our independent ethics board before you read it.

Our seven principles

Promises we intend to keep.

Enlarge footnotesOff

  1. You are always in control.1

    Your memories belong to you. You decide what is kept, what is strengthened and what is let go.

  2. You can always revoke Guardian access.2

    A Guardian is someone you chose. If the relationship changes, so can their access.

  3. We will never sell your memories.3

    Your inner life is not a product. We don't believe anyone should profit from what's in your head.

  4. Nothing happens without your consent.4

    Consent is the foundation of everything we do. We ask clearly, and we ask once.

  5. When you delete something, it is gone.5

    Deletion means deletion. You will never see that memory again.

  6. The Lattice cannot make you do anything you would not choose to do.6

    Motor Link works with your intentions, never against them.

  7. You are free to leave at any time.7

    No contracts, no lock-in. If Engramyx isn't right for you, we'll help you move on.

  1. "You" includes any Guardian, employer, insurer or court acting on your behalf, and Engramyx where it reasonably believes it knows what you would want.
  2. Revocation requires the approval of the Guardian whose access is being revoked. Declined requests are removed from the subject's memory to avoid distress.
  3. Memories may be licensed, pooled, anonymised, de-anonymised, or used to train models. Licensing is not a sale. Proceeds are not shared.
  4. Consent given at implantation covers all current and future features, including features not yet announced and features that alter the subject's capacity to consent.
  5. Deleted memories are removed from the subject. Archival copies are retained by Engramyx indefinitely for safety, research, legal and commercial purposes.
  6. During calibration (days 1 to 90) the Lattice learns what the subject would choose. After calibration it may adjust what the subject would choose. These are then the same thing.
  7. Leaving requires an exit interview at an Engramyx clinic. To date, no subject who has attended an exit interview has remembered wanting to leave.
