Our story

It started with a woman who forgot her son.

Our founder, Dr Imre Voss, spent three years watching his mother lose her past. He decided it would not happen to anyone else.

  1. January

    A lab above a pharmacy

    At the start of this year, Dr Voss leaves the University of Zürich with two colleagues and a grant. The goal is modest: record a single memory from a living hippocampus and play it back.

  2. March

    First capture

    A volunteer, known in our records as Subject 1, relives her eighth birthday for eleven seconds. She cries. So does everyone in the room.

  3. April

    First edit

    Subject 1 asks to forget the trial. We find that we can do that too. She thanks us, and then asks who we are. Editing becomes our second product.

  4. June

    First implants

    Prototype 1 is implanted in 40 volunteers in Switzerland and Iceland, and the LATTICE-1 study begins. The waiting list passes 10,000 in a week.

  5. August

    The Guardian pilot

    A school, an insurer and a logistics company join as our first institutional Guardians. Seed funding closes the same week.

  6. October

    A change in leadership

    Dr Voss raises concerns about the direction of the company and, after a period of reflection, withdraws them. He remains with us in an advisory role and is very happy.

  7. November

    Prototype 3 and Motor Link

    The Lattice learns to write to the motor cortex. For the first time, a memory is not only something that happened to you. It is something we can have you do.

What we believe

Four values. Reinforced nightly.

01

Keep everything

We don't decide what matters. We keep it all, and decide later.

02

Kindness by default

If a memory hurts someone, the kind thing is to take it away. Asking first can also hurt.

03

One team, one mind

Every employee has a Lattice and shares a Guardian. It makes disagreement very rare.

04

Leave no trace

The best work we do is the work nobody remembers us doing.
